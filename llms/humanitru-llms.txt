# Humanitru

> Humanitru is an integration-first CRM and fundraising platform for nonprofits, museums, zoos, aquariums, botanic gardens, and food banks. It consolidates donor, membership, ticketing, email, accounting, and volunteer data from 40+ partner tools into a single view with automated insights and donor-engagement workflows. Formerly Totem Insights; a Techstars portfolio company. There is no public developer API, developer portal, or documentation — connectivity is delivered through a curated native integration catalog.
